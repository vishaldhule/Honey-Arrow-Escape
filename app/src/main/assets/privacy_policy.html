🍯 PRIVACY POLICY

Honey Arrow Escape

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Summary: Honey Arrow Escape is designed with player privacy first. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information. All puzzle progress and coin balances remain strictly on your local device.

1. Introduction

Honey Arrow Escape ("we," "our," or "us") provides this Privacy Policy to inform you of our practices concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of information on Android devices.

2. Information Collection and Storage

We do not collect any personal information (such as your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or GPS location).

All gameplay data—including completed levels, star ratings, move counts, coin totals, and unlocked cartoon companions—is saved solely within local Android private storage (SharedPreferences and Room SQLite).

3. Permissions

The app only requests android.permission.VIBRATE to provide gentle tactile haptics during arrow tapping and puzzle victories. You can turn this off anytime in the in-game Settings.

4. Google Play Data Safety

  • No Personal Data Collected
  • No Data Shared with Third Parties
  • Offline Functionality: Core puzzle mechanics operate offline without external sync servers.
  • Data Deletion: Players can clear all stored progress anytime via Settings → Reset All Game Progress.

5. Children's Privacy

Honey Arrow Escape complies with COPPA and GDPR-K. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
