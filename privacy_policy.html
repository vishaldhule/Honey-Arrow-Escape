Privacy Policy for Honey Arrow Escape

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Summary: Honey Arrow Escape is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). All your game progress, level unlocks, and coin balances are stored strictly locally on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Honey Arrow Escape ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information when you play our game on Android devices.

By downloading, installing, or playing Honey Arrow Escape, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information Collection and Use

We believe in minimal data footprint. Honey Arrow Escape does not require any user account creation, registration, or login with personal credentials.

A. Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

We do not collect any personal information such as:

  • Your real name, address, or phone number
  • Your email address (unless you voluntarily contact our support team)
  • Precise or approximate GPS location data
  • Financial, banking, or credit card information
  • Biometric or health information
  • Contacts, photos, audio, or media stored on your device

B. Locally Stored Game Data

The application saves certain non-personal game progress data strictly on your device's local storage (via Android SharedPreferences and local Room SQLite database):

  • Highest unlocked puzzle level (Levels 1 to 1,000)
  • Star ratings (1, 2, or 3 stars) and best move records per completed level
  • In-game virtual coin balance and hint tokens
  • Unlocked cartoon mascot companions (Bee, Toby, Milo, Bao Bao, Leo, etc.)
  • Sound, music, and haptic feedback toggles
  • Consecutive daily login streak counters

Note: This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to external cloud databases.

3. Device Permissions Used

Honey Arrow Escape requests only the minimum necessary permissions strictly required for gameplay:

Permission Classification Purpose
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal / Install-time Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when tapping arrows, clearing grids, or encountering blockers. Can be disabled anytime in Settings.

We do not request sensitive or dangerous permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Read/Write External Storage, Location, or Contacts.

4. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In accordance with the Google Play Console Data Safety section requirements:

  • Data Collected: No user data is collected.
  • Data Shared: No user data is shared with third parties.
  • Security Practices: No data is transferred over an internet connection because the game operates completely offline.
  • Data Deletion Request: Players can erase all locally stored game data and statistics at any time by navigating to Settings → Reset All Game Progress within the app, or by clearing app storage in Android System Settings.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The core gameplay operates locally and offline. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, behavioral profiling engines, or targeted advertisement networks that monitor your personal browsing habits.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Honey Arrow Escape is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages, including children.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • If a parent or legal guardian discovers that their child has provided us with personal contact information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information promptly.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your game data. Because all progress is saved locally within your device's sandboxed private storage directory, other apps installed on your device cannot access your Honey Arrow Escape game records.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
